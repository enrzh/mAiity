# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using mAiity on web and iPhone to discover places, search addresses,
browse nearby categories, save locations, and navigate. They expect the map to
remain the primary surface and common actions to stay immediately available
across screen sizes and map providers.

## Product Purpose

mAiity provides a fast, map-first discovery and navigation experience with a
choice between native Apple Maps and custom visual map styles. Success means
that search, location, camera, saved places, and navigation behave predictably
regardless of the selected renderer.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, minimal, and dependable. The interface should feel native to its
platform without becoming inconsistent between web and iOS.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized panels, nested cards, decorative chrome, hidden core actions,
provider-specific control confusion, and search results trapped inside controls
that users must repeatedly expand.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the map as the primary working surface.
2. Preserve the user's layout and provider choices between sessions.
3. Give equivalent actions consistent placement and behavior across renderers.
4. Reveal contextual results without displacing or hiding the initiating control.
5. Prefer platform-native capabilities when they improve reliability or familiarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard support on web. Respect Dynamic Type,
VoiceOver, safe areas, reduced motion, and minimum 44-point touch targets on
iOS. Do not rely on color alone to communicate selection or state.
